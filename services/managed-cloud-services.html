OUR METHODOLOGY

How we operate

Automation-First Operations

We don't manage clouds manually. We implement Infrastructure-as-Code, automated remediation, self-healing systems, and policy engines to reduce human toil and eliminate manual errors.

SLA-Driven Accountability

Every engagement has defined SLAs: response time (target 15 min), resolution time (target <30 min for critical incidents), uptime targets, and cost optimization targets. You get monthly dashboards proving delivery.

Named Principal Model

You don't get a ticket queue. You get a named engineer who is accountable for your environment. They own the relationship, the runbooks, the knowledge, and the delivery.

Proactive, Not Reactive

We monitor and optimize continuously. We alert before failures happen. We recommend changes before problems occur. You'll know about issues before your users do.

Documented Everything

If it's in one person's head, it's a risk. Every runbook, every procedure, every decision is documented. Your team can always understand what's running and why.

FAQ

Common questions

How do you price managed cloud services?

We use a flat monthly fee model, tiered by cloud complexity. Pricing is based on the number of accounts, services, and the level of optimization required — not hours consumed. A typical engagement ranges from $5K–$25K/month. Every quote includes a fixed 2-week Discover phase so we can give you an accurate estimate.

What's the minimum engagement length?

We ask for a 12-month commitment to managed services. This allows us to stabilize your environment, implement long-term optimizations, and deliver real ROI. Shorter engagements (90 days) are available for Discover + Design phases if you want to test the waters first.

Do you work with existing AWS/Azure/GCP implementations?

Yes. We specialize in this. We can take over existing architectures, rationalize them, optimize costs, plug security gaps, and operationalize them. We don't require a greenfield build. In fact, most of our clients come to us because their existing setup is messy or expensive.

How is 24×7 coverage actually staffed?

We maintain a distributed on-call team across North American time zones. For critical incidents, we have engineers available at any time. We don't use offshore-only support. Your named principal is always accessible, and escalation paths are clear and fast.

What kind of cost savings should we expect?

Average first-year savings are 20–35% through FinOps optimization. This comes from rightsizing, reservation strategies, waste elimination, and architecture improvements. Savings vary by current state (a well-optimized environment saves less; a sprawling one saves more). We show you every dollar saved with detailed monthly reports.

How do you handle compliance and security?

We implement architecture and controls aligned to CIS benchmarks, SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA-ready standards (depending on your needs). We harden IAM, encrypt data, implement secrets management, set up logging and monitoring, and work with your auditors during compliance reviews. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are not optional—they're built into every engagement.

What happens if you go out of business?

We maintain runbooks and documentation so your team can take over if needed. During the Discover phase, we ensure every process is documented and your team is trained. We also provide 60 days of transition support and source code/configuration backups. Your data and architecture remain yours.
